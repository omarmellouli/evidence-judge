Evidence Judge

Multimodal RAG often gives the right answer from the wrong evidence. This demo walks one question through my pipeline: vector search finds five candidates, hypergraph facts describe each one, and an LLM judge reads those facts and decides which evidence actually answers the question.

Omar Mellouli · Diagnosing and Improving Low Retrieval Precision in Multimodal RAG via Agentic-based Multimodal HypergraphRAG

Try it yourself · two real models

Your question, your evidence, your images

Stage 1 is an independent embedding model, all-MiniLM-L6-v2, that runs entirely in your browser: no account, no server. Stage 2 is the judge, by default the open-source Qwen2.5 model, also running in your browser on the GPU. Add images to the question or to any candidate: a vision model turns them into text first, as in my thesis (images as text, not CLIP).

Precision@1: is the first evidence the right one?

98.6%right evidence somewhere in the top-5 after data preparation, but only 78.8% at rank 1. Ranking, not search, is the bottleneck.
+5.7 / +7.7 ppP@1 from the judge on ScienceQA-212 and SulzerDataset. P@5 unchanged: it reorders, it never loses the right text.
11–14 sper query with the judge (×2.4–2.8 latency), under 1 ¢ per precision point.
+0.28 ppon the full ScienceQA-2120 corpus: reasoning works but is not yet efficient at scale. This is where my PhD direction starts.
The three examples are illustrative, written in the style of the thesis data (EHRXQA/MedHyperGraph, SulzerDataset, ScienceQA); they are not taken from the real corpora. The report thumbnails reuse the one sample X-ray from MedHyperGraph Fig. 1; the other thumbnails are drawn icons. Their similarity scores are real: computed with all-MiniLM-L6-v2, the same model the live panel runs. The thesis itself used OpenAI text-embedding-3-large and GPT-4.1. The potassium example in “Try it yourself” is also illustrative; its lab chart is drawn for this demo. All numbers in the results section are the measured thesis results. Embedding model: sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (Apache-2.0), int8 weights from the Xenova ONNX build, run here in plain JavaScript. Open-source judge: Qwen2.5-Instruct (Apache-2.0) and Phi-3.5-vision (MIT) through WebLLM, or Qwen2.5-VL through Ollama.
